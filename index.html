Questions, answered

How is Nodelay different from a regular database?

Nodelay sits alongside your existing database as a real-time sync layer, so you don't have to build your own websocket infrastructure or polling logic.

Enterprise plans support dedicated and self-hosted deployments. Contact sales to discuss your requirements.

What happens if I go over my plan's event limit?

You'll get a heads-up before you hit your limit, and can upgrade at any time — we never drop your traffic without warning.
